CNN Cat Dog Classification - Training Experiments

========================================
Experiment 1: Baseline CNN
========================================

Configuration:
- Input size: 224x224
- Batch size: 32
- Optimizer: Adam
- Learning rate: 0.001
- Dropout: None
- Data augmentation: RandomHorizontalFlip

Epoch    Loss      Validation Accuracy
1        0.6359    0.7056
2        0.5212    0.7498
3        0.4257    0.7474
4        0.3154    0.7604
5        0.1854    0.7576
6        0.0883    0.7528
7        0.0434    0.7368
8        0.0328    0.7448
9        0.0253    0.7328
10       0.0165    0.7446

Best Validation Accuracy: 76.04%
Best Epoch: 4

Observation:
Training loss continued to decrease, while validation accuracy
peaked around Epoch 4 and then declined. This suggests overfitting.


========================================
Experiment 2: CNN + Dropout
========================================

Configuration:
- Input size: 224x224
- Batch size: 32
- Optimizer: Adam
- Learning rate: 0.001
- Dropout: 0.5
- Data augmentation: RandomHorizontalFlip

Epoch    Loss      Validation Accuracy
1        0.6563    0.6858
2        0.5634    0.7420
3        0.5170    0.7570
4        0.4820    0.7658
5        0.4527    0.7764
6        0.4203    0.7836
7        0.3988    0.7946
8        0.3684    0.7816
9        0.3438    0.7876
10       0.3196    0.7964

Best Validation Accuracy: 79.64%
Best Epoch: 10

Observation:
Compared with the baseline experiment, validation accuracy reached
79.64% with Dropout=0.5.

Note:
The random train/validation split was not fixed with a random seed,
so the two experiments did not necessarily use exactly the same
validation samples. Therefore, the accuracy difference should not
be interpreted as a perfectly controlled Dropout comparison.
